Colours nouns in the subtitles by their grammatical gender, and articles like their noun. The gender comes from your offline dictionaries, so it works for any language whose dictionary marks it (choose them below): the wty- dictionaries (e.g. wty-de-en, wty-fr-en, wty-ru-en) do, and so do the older kty- ones, a little less precisely. German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese also get their articles coloured; other languages get the nouns.
Plain (SRT, VTT) subtitles only: not ASS subtitles, and not when mpv draws the subtitles. Turn it on and off while watching with {{ ctrl }}+Shift+G.

Only subtitles in these languages are coloured (the project's subtitle language); words in any other language are never looked up. The defaults are the languages it was tested with. Add another one if your dictionary for it marks the gender of nouns (Czech, Ukrainian...); languages without grammatical gender, like English or Japanese, have nothing to colour.
