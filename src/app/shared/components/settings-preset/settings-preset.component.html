Standard media player behavior.

Video plays continuously without automatic pauses. Subtitles are shown or hidden according to your manual toggle.

} @else if (preset.id === BuiltInSettingsPreset.LISTENING) {

Clips play without subtitles and pause automatically at the end.

Goal: Listen and figure out what was said.

  • Not sure? Press ↑ to replay the audio.
  • Ready? Press ↓ or C to reveal subtitles and check your answer.

To continue, press Spacebar (unpause) or ↓ again (force continue).

} @else if (preset.id === BuiltInSettingsPreset.SPEAKING) {

Clips start paused automatically and with subtitles visible.

Goal: Read the subtitles out loud and practice your pronunciation.

  • Press Spacebar (unpause) to hear the native audio and compare it with your speech.
  • The clip automatically pauses at the end so you can press ↑ to replay and perfect your accent.

To continue, press Spacebar (unpause) or ↓ (force continue).

} @else if (preset.id === BuiltInSettingsPreset.SHADOWING) {

Clips play automatically with subtitles visible.

Goal: Shadow the audio (speak along with it).

  • Audio plays immediately upon entering the clip.
  • The clip automatically pauses at the end so you can repeat it with ↑.

To continue, press Spacebar (unpause) or ↓ (force continue).
